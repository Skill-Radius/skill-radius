Where's the ID?

At the top right of every SkillRadius certificate, and in the footer next to the signatures. It starts with CERT-SR.

${I2}

What does "valid" mean?

Professional certifications are valid for 2–3 years from the exam date. Course certificates of completion don't expire.

Something looks wrong?

Email support@skillradius.in with the ID and we'll confirm the record within one working day.
