1. Overview

We want you to be happy with every purchase. This policy explains when you can cancel an order and get your money back. It applies to all purchases made on www.skillradius.in and should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Self-paced courses

  • 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: You can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase if you have completed less than 30% of the course content.
  • Progress is measured by completed lessons in your dashboard.
  • Courses marked as “non-refundable” on the course page are exempt from this policy.

3. Live trainings

  • Full refund: if you cancel at least 3 days before the batch start date.
  • Batch transfer: within 3 days of the start date, you can move to a later batch once, free of charge, instead of a refund.
  • After the second session of a batch, fees are not refundable.
  • In-person workshops: full refund up to 3 days before the workshop. Materials already shipped are not refundable.

4. Professional certifications

  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not attempted the proctored exam.
  • Once you have attempted the exam, the certification fee is not refundable. Retakes are always free.

5. Learning paths and bundles

Bundles are refunded per component: each course, training and certification in the bundle follows its own rule above, at its share of the bundle price.

6. How to request a refund

  • From your dashboard: go to My Learning → Orders and click Request refund on the order.
  • By email: write to support@skillradius.in with your order ID (it starts with SR-ORD).
  • We confirm eligibility within 2 working days.

7. When you'll get your money

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method. Your bank may take a few more days to show the credit. Access to the refunded content is removed once the refund is approved.

8. Failed or duplicate payments

If money was deducted but your order failed, or you were charged twice, the extra amount is reversed automatically within 5–7 working days. If it isn't, raise a ticket with the transaction ID.

9. Cancellations by SkillRadius

If we cancel or reschedule a batch or event, you can choose a full refund or a free transfer to another batch.
