1. Our goal

SkillRadius should work for every learner, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, captions or other assistive technology. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

2. What we do

  • Every page can be used with a keyboard, with visible focus outlines.
  • Images that carry meaning have text descriptions; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text and buttons meet colour-contrast guidelines, and pages zoom to 200% without losing content.
  • Animations switch off automatically when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Forms explain errors in plain words next to the field that needs attention.

3. Courses and live sessions

  • Course videos have closed captions, and lesson notes are available as text.
  • Live sessions are recorded, so you can re-watch at your own pace.
  • Trainers can share slides in advance on request.

4. Exams

If you need extra time, a scribe, a screen reader or another adjustment for a proctored certification exam, write to us at least 7 days before your exam. Adjustments are free.

5. Known limitations

  • Some older course videos are still being re-captioned. Captions for all videos will be complete by the end of the current quarter.
  • Third-party payment pages are provided by our payment partners and follow their own accessibility standards.

6. Ask for support

If something on SkillRadius is hard to use, tell us. We reply within two working days.
