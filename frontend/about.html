About SkillRadius

Learning that leads to real outcomes

SkillRadius is designed to make modern learning simple, engaging, and results-driven. We combine expert-led content, interactive learning tools, and a supportive community to help learners achieve real outcomes.

Our mission

Close the gap between what people learn and what work needs

We started SkillRadius in Bengaluru because too many capable people were stuck between theory-heavy degrees and jobs that wanted proof of skill.

So every course is built around real projects, every live batch is taught by people who do the work every day, and every certification ends in a proctored exam that employers can verify.
