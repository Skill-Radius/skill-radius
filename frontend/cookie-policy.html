1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (like browser local storage) are small pieces of data saved on your device. They help a website remember who you are and what you were doing.

2. How we use them

In line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we only use non-essential cookies with your consent.

  • Essential: keep you signed in, remember your course progress, cart and cookie choices. These can't be switched off.
  • Analytics: tell us which pages and lessons are used, so we can improve them. Off unless you agree.
  • Marketing: help us show relevant SkillRadius offers on other websites. Off unless you agree.

3. What we store on your device

  • sr_user: your signed-in session
  • sr_progress, sr_enrolled: your learning progress and enrolments
  • sr_consent: your cookie choices
  • sr_orders, sr_wishlist: your orders and saved courses

4. Changing your choices

You can change your choices at any time using Cookie settings in the website footer, or by clearing site data in your browser. Clearing site data will also sign you out.

5. Third parties

Payment pages are provided by our payment partners and set their own cookies. Embedded content like fonts may be loaded from third-party services.

6. Changes to this policy

We'll update the date at the top of this page when we change this policy, and tell you about significant changes.
