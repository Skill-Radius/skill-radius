1. Digital delivery

SkillRadius sells digital learning services. We do not ship physical goods, except optional printed certificates described below. Everything you buy is delivered to your SkillRadius account.

2. Self-paced courses and certifications

  • Access is delivered instantly once your payment is confirmed.
  • You'll receive an order confirmation and GST invoice by email within 15 minutes.
  • Your course or certification appears immediately under My Learning.

3. Live trainings

  • Your seat is confirmed instantly and appears under My Learning → Trainings.
  • Joining instructions are emailed 7 days before the batch starts.
  • The Join button for each live session appears on your dashboard 15 minutes before it starts.
  • Recordings are posted within a few hours of each session.

4. In-person workshops

Venue details, timings and what to bring are emailed 7 days before the workshop and shown on the workshop page.

5. Certificates

  • Digital certificates are issued instantly when you complete a course or pass a certification exam, and can be downloaded as a PDF.
  • Printed certificates (optional, on request) are dispatched within 3 working days and delivered within 7–10 working days to addresses in India.

6. If something doesn't arrive

If you can't see your purchase in My Learning within 2 hours of paying, or didn't receive your confirmation email, raise a ticket or write to support@skillradius.in with your order ID. We'll fix it within one working day.
