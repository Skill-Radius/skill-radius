How we hire

From application to offer in about two weeks

Four steps, clear timelines, and feedback whether or not it works out.

1

Apply

Send your profile and a line on why the role fits. We reply to every application within 5 working days.

2

Intro call

A 30-minute chat with the hiring manager about the role, the team and what you want next.

3

Skills task

A short, paid take-home or a live session close to the real work. No trick puzzles.

4

Meet the team

Talk to future teammates, then get a written offer with salary, ESOPs and joining date.
