1. Our commitment

We want every learner, trainer and partner to be treated fairly. This page explains how to raise a complaint about our courses, live trainings, certifications, payments, content or the handling of your personal data, in line with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Before you escalate

Most questions are solved fastest by our learner support team. Raise a ticket from Support in your account, or contact us. If the answer doesn't resolve your issue, raise a formal grievance as described below.

3. Raise a grievance

  • Online: raise a support ticket and choose the topic “Grievance / complaint”.
  • Email: grievance@skillradius.in, with the subject line “Grievance”.
  • Post: to the Grievance Officer at the address below.

4. Grievance Officer

The Grievance Officer is responsible for receiving and resolving complaints.

  • Email: grievance@skillradius.in
  • Phone: +91 98765 43210 (Mon–Sat, 9:30 am – 7:00 pm IST)
  • Address: Cinnabar Hills, Embassy Golf Links Business Park, Challaghatta, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560071, India

5. Timelines

  • We acknowledge every grievance within 24 hours and give you a reference number.
  • We resolve it within 15 days of receipt. If we need more time, we'll tell you why and when to expect an answer.
  • Requests to remove content that is unlawful or that impersonates you are handled within 72 hours.

6. What to include

  • Your name, the email on your SkillRadius account and a phone number
  • The order ID, certificate ID or batch name, if relevant
  • What happened, and what outcome you are asking for
  • Screenshots or documents that help us understand the issue

7. If you are not satisfied

If you are not satisfied with our decision, you may approach the National Consumer Helpline (dial 1915 or visit consumerhelpline.gov.in), or the appropriate authority under applicable law. Our Refund Policy and Terms & Conditions also apply.
